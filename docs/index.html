    How it works

    See. Think. Coach. Step back.

    1

    Sees

    A camera watches the routine. A vision-language model (Claude Opus 5.5) checks whether the current step is done, in progress, or off track. Frames are never stored.

    2

    Coaches

    An LLM writes short, kind prompts in Kuwaiti Arabic. Gulf-Arabic speech recognition and text-to-speech make it feel like talking to a friend.

    3

    Steps back

    A prompt-fading algorithm gives less help each week, and calm mode reacts to early signs of overload. The goal: the child needs Rafeeq less over time.

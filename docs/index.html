Three things a picture schedule can't do

It sees

Gemma 3 checks one camera frame every few seconds: is this step done? Then it gives the next small step, or a hint.

It calms

Too loud, or heart rate climbing? Instructions stop. Soft voice, blue eyes and a breathing game, then back to the routine.

It steps back

After three good mornings, it says less on that step: full guidance, then a hint, then silence. The goal is a child who doesn't need it.

Questions

Where do the camera pictures go?

Nowhere. Each frame is checked on the device and deleted on the spot. No cloud, no internet needed.

Is it trying to make autistic kids "normal"?

No. It only helps with self-care steps the family chooses, like brushing teeth, and it never scolds.

What if the AI makes a mistake?

It will, sometimes. The worst case is one extra hint. It never punishes, and parents can correct anything.

Does it replace parents or therapists?

No. They decide what the child learns. Rafeeq helps the child practise at home, every morning.

1. What Rafeeq is

Rafeeq is a student project and prototype by A.Naji, made for the ATSN26 Gen-AI & Software Innovation Competition in Kuwait. It is not a finished product, and it is not for sale.

2. Not a medical device

  • Rafeeq is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat or cure autism, Down syndrome, ADHD or any other condition.
  • It does not replace doctors, therapists, teachers or parents. Always follow the advice of the professionals who know your child.
  • The Rafeeq Band's heart-rate feature is a comfort tool, not a health monitor. Don't rely on it to detect medical problems.

3. An adult stays in charge

  • The demo and the robot must be set up and supervised by a parent or carer. Never leave a child alone with it.
  • Stop using it straight away if it upsets the child. Some children don't like wearing a band, being watched by a camera, or hearing a voice. That's normal.
  • AI can make mistakes. Rafeeq can misread a step or say something that doesn't fit. Check that it behaves well before using it with a child.

4. The simulation and the demo video

Privacy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Rafeeq is a student project by A.Naji for the ATSN26 Gen-AI & Software Innovation Competition in Kuwait. This page covers two things: this website, and the Rafeeq robot as it is designed.

This website

  • No accounts, no sign-up, no forms.
  • No analytics, no ads, no tracking pixels, no cookies.
  • Fonts, audio and video are served from this site itself, not from Google or other third parties.
  • Your browser remembers one thing on your own device: whether you picked English or Arabic. You can clear it any time by clearing site data.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep basic server logs, such as IP addresses, to run and secure its service. See GitHub's privacy statement.

The simulation

The simulation runs entirely in your browser. Rafeeq's answers in it are scripted to show how the real system behaves. Nothing you click is sent anywhere.

The Rafeeq robot (design)

  • The AI model (Gemma 3) runs on the device in the family's home. It does not need the internet.
  • Camera frames are checked one at a time and deleted right after. Video is never recorded or uploaded.
  • The parent app shows a text summary of the routine (steps done, hints, calm-downs), not pictures.
  • Parents choose the routines and can switch the camera off at any time.

Children

This website does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Contact

Questions? Open an issue on the project's GitHub page.
